## Per-card content

### 1. Hotel property photo → premium campaign
- **LinkedIn caption:** "Same property. Different perceived value. Most hotels don't need a new photo shoot — they need the assets they already own finished properly. One polish pass, one campaign frame, and the room finally looks like what it costs."
- **Website card copy:** A photo in a folder becomes a campaign asset that earns the rate.
- **Sales-call talking point:** "This took under an hour, no shoot. Imagine it weekly, across your portfolio."
- **Dimensions:** pair at 1080×1350 each (4:5); side-by-side composite 1600×1000.
- **Design style:** editorial photo treatment, warm golden grade, thin gold divider between before/after, serif headline.

### 2. Menu item / F&B special → restaurant campaign
- **LinkedIn caption:** "Hotel restaurants are often the most under-marketed asset on property. This started as a phone photo from the line — now it sells the special while it's still on the menu."
- **Website card:** From the pass to the feed, before the special changes.
- **Talking point:** "Your chef already shoots this on a phone. We finish it the same week."
- **Dimensions:** 1080×1350 graphic; 1080×1920 story; motion 9:16, 8–12s.
- **Design style:** appetite-first: rich color/texture work, bold price/offer framing, dark backdrop with gold accent.

### 3. Local event → hotel booking campaign
- **LinkedIn caption:** "A local event is not just an event — it's a booking reason. Most hotels post about it after it sells out. The campaign belongs inside the booking window."
- **Website card:** Every nearby event, turned into a reason to stay with you.
- **Talking point:** "What's the biggest event near your property in the next 90 days? That's month one of the pilot."
- **Dimensions:** 1080×1350 feed + 1080×1920 story pair.
- **Design style:** event-poster energy with hotel brand discipline: date lockup, property photo base, gold CTA band.

### 4. Plain schedule → branded monthly calendar
- **LinkedIn caption:** "Nobody screenshots a paragraph. Turned a text schedule into the most-saved asset this property posts — same information, finished format."
- **Website card:** The whole month's programming in one asset worth saving.
- **Talking point:** "This one gets reused twelve times a year. It pays for its design in month one."
- **Dimensions:** 1080×1350 feed; 1080×1920 story crop; printable A4 variant for on-property.
- **Design style:** clean grid calendar, brand fonts, gold date markers, one hero image strip.

### 5. Spa service → wellness campaign creative
- **LinkedIn caption:** "Spas sell calm. The creative has to feel like the treatment room — soft light, quiet type, no noise. This is what that looks like."
- **Website card:** Wellness creative that finally matches the experience.
- **Talking point:** "Gift-card season is a revenue spike most hotel spas meet with a text post."
- **Dimensions:** 1080×1350; story 1080×1920; soft-motion loop 4:5, 6–8s.
- **Design style:** desaturated warm neutrals, generous whitespace, thin-serif type, minimal gold.

### 6. Basic brand asset → polished campaign system
- **LinkedIn caption:** "One idea became five assets: feed graphic, reel cover, story, promo card, caption direction. That's the difference between a graphic and a system."
- **Website card:** One idea in, a coordinated campaign set out.
- **Talking point:** "You don't buy assets from me — you buy the system that multiplies them."
- **Dimensions:** system board composite 1600×2000 showing 1080×1350 feed, 1080×1920 story, 1080×1080 reel cover.
- **Design style:** mockup-board layout on dark canvas, each asset framed with thin gold rules.
