# 3–5 Property Creative Pilot — Reusable 6-Slide Deck

> Reusable skeleton + per-account variants. Customize slide 1 with real research
> before every send. Never invent facts about a prospect — [brackets] = verify first.

---

## The 6 slides (master copy)

### Slide 1 — Custom company intro
**Title:** [Company] — [N] properties, one creative standard.
**Copy:** "You operate [N] properties across [brands/markets]. We looked at how the portfolio shows up online — here's what we noticed, and what 3–5 of your properties could look like with a dedicated creative system behind them."
*(One screenshot row of their actual property feeds. Praise something real first.)*

### Slide 2 — The problem
**Title:** Property-level creative gets inconsistent at scale.
**Copy:** "Corporate sets the brand. Each property executes with whoever has a spare hour. The result: different templates, different quality, different cadence — and a portfolio that reads smaller than it is. It's not a talent problem. It's a bandwidth problem."

### Slide 3 — The opportunity
**Title:** Stronger visuals support perceived value and demand.
**Copy:** "Guests can't walk your lobby from a phone — they feel the property through its creative. Consistent, premium property-level creative supports rate integrity, direct-booking interest, F&B covers, and event inquiries. Same properties, higher perceived value per scroll."

### Slide 4 — Proof
**Title:** Built for hospitality, measured in the open.
**Copy:** "13.9M+ impressions · 543K+ direct engagements · 3.6M+ reach · 2.4K+ creative assets delivered. Current and past work: Hotel Indigo Pittsburgh, Hampton Inn properties (brand-standard-aware), Eliza Hot Metal Bistro (hotel F&B), spa/wellness and event campaigns."
*(2–3 before/after pairs matched to their category — see proof library.)*

### Slide 5 — What the pilot includes
**Title:** The 3–5 property pilot.
**Copy:** "One month, 3–5 of your properties: monthly creative plan per property · social graphics · short-form motion · F&B/event/wedding promos · photo polishing · new branded creative · captions · one approval workflow, one invoice. 3-property pilots from $4,500/mo; 5-property portfolio pilots from $10,000/mo; creative + local SEO scoped $7,500–$15,000/mo."

### Slide 6 — Next step
**Title:** Pick the 3–5 properties.
**Copy:** "We suggest [Property A, B, C — most photogenic + one F&B-heavy]. Kickoff is a 30-minute call: brand files, approval contact, what's coming on the calendar. First assets within the first week. Expansion across the portfolio happens on evidence, not promises."

## Per-account slide-1 variants

### Island Hospitality (multi-property management co)
"Island manages a large portfolio of recognizable hotel brands across markets. At that scale, property-level creative consistency is a corporate asset — it shows in every owner review and brand audit. Here's what a 3–5 property creative pilot would look like inside Island's portfolio." *(Use public info only; verify portfolio details before sending.)*

### MCR Hotels (large owner-operator, efficiency culture)
"MCR's model is famous for operational discipline. Creative is usually the line that discipline misses — every property improvising its own social. A fixed-fee creative system is the operationally boring answer: predictable cost, predictable output, no headcount. Here's the 3–5 property version."

### Auberge / luxury group
"At the luxury tier, the gap between the in-person experience and the feed is the most expensive gap in hospitality. Your properties sell a feeling; the creative has to carry it. This is a quiet, taste-first system: photo polish, restrained motion, editorial-grade campaign assets — scoped for [3–5 named properties]."

### Regional hotel management company
"You run [N] select-service and full-service properties across [region]. None of them justifies a creative hire; all of them need consistent output. One outside system covers the set — brand-standard-aware for the flags, custom for the independents — on one invoice."

### Restaurant/F&B-heavy hotel group
"Your restaurants and bars are the most under-marketed assets in the portfolio — local revenue the rooms team never sees. The pilot weights toward F&B: menu features, bar programs, event pushes, plus rooms-and-amenities coverage. [Restaurant names] each get their own voice without a second team."

## Follow-up sequence after sending the deck

1. **Same day:** "Just sent the pilot overview over — the part most groups react to is slide [5/the pilot scope]. Curious which 3–5 properties you'd pick if you ran it."
2. **Day 2:** "One thought since sending: [specific idea for one of their properties — a season, event, or F&B push]. That's the kind of thing the pilot would cover in month one."
3. **Day 5:** "Any reaction to the pilot overview? Happy to tailor it to specific properties if that's more useful than the general version."
4. **Close the loop:** "Don't want to clutter your inbox — should I close the loop on this for now, or is it a timing thing?"
5. **Routing:** "Who else should see this? If creative sits with someone else — corporate marketing, digital, commercial strategy — happy to send them the deck directly."
6. **Smaller pilot:** "Would a smaller version make more sense as a first step — even a single flagship property for one month? Same system, lower commitment, same proof."

*(All sends are manual. Log every touch in the CRM; save replies to Winning Messages.)*
