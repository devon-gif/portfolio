## What still needs booking/inquiry tracking

- Campaign-specific landing pages with form submissions

- Inquiry form data from hotel/restaurant websites
- OpenTable/Resy reservation counts tied to promoted dates
- Event RSVP or ticket sales connected to promoted events

## How to explain this on sales calls

**Opening:**
"We track across all our active profiles through SHAIPE. The grouped report shows 14.8M+ impressions, 565K+ direct engagements, 4.3M+ reach, and a 3.8% engagement rate across hotel, restaurant, event, and wellness campaigns since late 2020."

**When asked about bookings:**
"The data shows strong reach, impressions, engagement, shares, and comments, which tells us the creative is creating real attention. Direct booking attribution depends on the property's own tracking setup. In a pilot, we'd add UTM links, campaign links, and any booking-signal tracking the property can share access to, so you have cleaner measurement beyond engagement."

**When asked which client:**
"The grouped report covers multiple tracked profiles: Eliza PGH, Hotel Indigo Pittsburgh, Hampton Inn Greensburg, Elements Salon & Wellness, and other properties. I can break down the per-profile numbers if helpful."

**On pilot scope:**
"We recommend starting with 3–5 properties, proving the workflow and creative quality, then expanding on evidence. The pilot period is also when we set up cleaner tracking so results are measurable beyond social performance."
