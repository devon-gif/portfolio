# Anti-AI Writing Style — Banned & Avoid List

> **Global rule:** Claude reads this file before writing ANYTHING public-facing (messages, comments, proposals, cover letters). The goal is output that never reads as generic AI.

## Hard-banned words/phrases (never use)
- game-changer / game-changing
- revolutionary
- unlock
- elevate
- delve
- tapestry
- synergy
- robust
- seamless
- transform your business / transform your brand
- "in today's fast-paced world"
- "I hope this message finds you well"
- "I wanted to reach out"
- "I'd love to pick your brain"
- "Dear [Name],"

## Use only if truly natural
- **leverage** — only in its plain sense ("you could leverage the photos you already have"), never as buzzword filler. When in doubt, use "use."

## Structural things to avoid
- **Generic intros.** No "As a creative professional…" or "In the hospitality industry…" openers. Start with a real detail about them.
- **Excessive bold.** Almost none in messages. Plain text.
- **Long explanations in LinkedIn DMs.** Keep DMs to 3–6 sentences.
- **Fake urgency.** No "limited spots," "act now," "only this week."
- **Sounding desperate.** No double/triple follow-ups, no guilt, no "just checking in again…"
- **Mass-agency pitch energy.** No "we offer a full suite of services," no feature lists, no "our team."
- **Template skeletons.** Vary openings and sentence length every time.
- **Three-part value stacks.** One idea per message.

## The tells that scream "AI wrote this"
- Perfectly balanced, same-length sentences.
- Every paragraph starts the same way.
- Em-dashes used in a tidy, repetitive rhythm.
- Summarizing what I just said back to me.
- A neat "In conclusion" / "I hope this helps" wrapper.
- Listing benefits in parallel structure.

## The fix
Write like a real person who did 90 seconds of homework on this specific property and is sending a quick, helpful note. Specific > polished. Short > complete. Human > correct.
