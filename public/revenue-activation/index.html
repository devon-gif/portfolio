Common questions

Before you ask.

Who do we work with day to day?+

Directly with Wesam on hotel sales and Devon on creative execution. The people introduced during the strategy call remain involved in the work.

What do you need from our property teams?+

A kickoff conversation, access to approved brand assets and property photography, current commercial priorities, and one point of contact for approvals. The working rhythm is designed to avoid unnecessary meetings for property leaders.

What happens in the first two weeks?+

HSC begins market and account mapping while Archer organizes the approved property assets and builds the first creative priorities. The initial month establishes the sales pipeline, reporting rhythm, and creative queue.

What happens at day 90?+

You receive a clear review of outreach activity, pipeline created, opportunities in motion, creative delivered, campaign support, and what can honestly be attributed. You then decide whether to scale, continue, adjust, or stop.

Are we locked in?+

The initial pilot is a defined 90-day engagement. Continuing beyond the pilot requires a new decision or written extension. Final client contract terms control.

Why not hire a sales contractor and a creative agency separately?+

The difference is the working rhythm. Sales priorities inform the creative queue, so the collateral, campaigns, and property content support the accounts and demand HSC is actively pursuing.
