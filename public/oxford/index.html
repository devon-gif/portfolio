The opportunity

Oxford already has the experiences. Archer helps create more usable marketing from them.

The idea is not a rebrand or another strategy layer. It is flexible creative production that can plug into the priorities Oxford’s marketing and property teams already have.

01 · Property

Hotel & destination creative

Social graphics, packages, local-demand campaigns, room and amenity promotion, direct-booking visuals and adaptations for individual properties.

02 · Experiences

F&B, rooftop, wedding & event support

Recurring assets for restaurants, bars, weddings, meetings, events and seasonal moments — the kinds of offers that need fresh creative throughout the year.

03 · Motion

Existing photography, brought to life

Turn approved hotel photography into polished short-form motion, transitions and campaign content so the asset library works harder between photo shoots.

How this could work

Start small enough to judge the work, then expand only if it helps.

Oxford provides

  • Approved property photography and brand standards
  • Current offers, priorities and campaign dates
  • A clear review/approval owner
  • Property information and approved claims

Archer produces

  • Static campaign and social graphics
  • Short-form motion and photo-to-motion pieces
  • F&B, event, wedding and seasonal creative
  • Multi-format adaptations and organized delivery
Simple pilot

One or two properties. One real campaign need.

Choose a property with an active need period, restaurant push, seasonal campaign or event calendar. Archer produces against that real brief, Oxford judges the quality and workflow, and there is no need to force a larger commitment if it is not useful.
