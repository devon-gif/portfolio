## 2. Section-by-section layout notes

1. **Hero** — full-height, near-black background with a faint gold radial glow top-center. Eyebrow (gold, letter-spaced) → large ivory headline → muted subheadline → two CTAs (primary = gold gradient fill, secondary = gold outline ghost). Availability line small and muted under CTAs. Optional thin gold divider at the bottom.
2. **Proof strip** — horizontal band on a slightly lighter card tone. 5 metrics in a row (gold numbers, muted labels). Client caption centered below. On scroll, numbers can count up.
3. **Portfolio** — 3-across card grid (image-led), gold hairline borders, soft hover lift + glow. Tag chips per card. Keep imagery editorial, warm-toned.
4. **Cost comparison** — two side-by-side cards: left "in-house hire" (muted, heavier), right "Archer Design system" (gold-accented, glowing). Big closing line centered beneath.
5. **Packages** — grouped by category (Hotel / Spa / Restaurant) with a "Custom Group" card. Featured card (Bundle) gets the gold gradient border. Price prominent in gold.
6. **What we create** — 6 cards in a 3×2 grid, small gold line-icon, title, one line each.
7. **Who it's for** — compact 2- or 3-column list with gold check/diamond bullets.
8. **Trial** — centered feature block on a darker panel with a gold border + glow; checklist of inclusions; single CTA.
9. **Process** — 4 numbered steps, horizontal on desktop, gold step numerals.
10. **FAQ** — accordion, gold dividers, ivory questions, muted answers.
11. **Final CTA** — full-width gold-gradient-edged panel, big headline, one CTA, studio line as a quiet footer note.

Global: generous vertical spacing, thin gold dividers between sections, sticky slim header (logo "Archer Design" left, nav + "Request a 7-Day Trial" button right).

## 3. Suggested Framer visual direction

- **Colors** — Background `#050505`/`#0B0B0A`; cards `#11100E`/`#17140F`; gold `#C9A44C`; soft gold `#E8D7A2`; ivory text `#F6F1E7`; muted `#A9A092`; borders `rgba(201,164,76,0.24)`; accent gradient `linear-gradient(135deg,#E8D7A2,#C9A44C,#8B6A21)`.
- **Type** — Editorial serif for headlines (e.g. Cormorant, Canela, or Playfair) in ivory; clean sans for body/UI (e.g. Inter, Geist). Generous line-height. Gold, letter-spaced uppercase eyebrows.
- **Texture & depth** — subtle vignette/grain on the background; soft gold radial glows behind hero + final CTA; 1px gold borders on cards; gentle shadow + glow on hover. Keep it calm — no neon, no glassmorphism overload, no tech-bro gradients.
- **Imagery** — warm, low-lit, editorial hospitality (lobbies, cocktail bars, plated F&B, spa, golden-hour exteriors). Apply a consistent warm/gold grade.
- **Buttons** — primary: gold gradient fill, near-black text, soft glow on hover. Secondary: transparent with gold border + gold text.
- **Motion** — restrained: fade/slide-up on scroll, count-up metrics, slow hover lifts. Nothing bouncy.

## 5. FAQ copy

**Do you need us to do a photo or video shoot?**
No. We work from the assets you already have — property photos, menus, event content, brand files. That's what keeps it efficient and affordable.

**What exactly do we get each month?**
A consistent set of finished, on-brand assets — social graphics, short-form motion, F&B/event promos, and seasonal campaigns — plus a simple monthly plan. SEO packages add local search content. Exact volume depends on the package.

**How is this cheaper than hiring someone?**
One in-house creative hire carries salary, benefits, software, recruiting, and management — usually six figures a year, loaded. A monthly package gives you the output without any of that overhead.

**Do you manage our social accounts or run ads?**
No. We create the content; you (or your team) post it. Paid ad management and full account management aren't included.

**How does the 7-day trial work?**
Send your existing assets and we'll create 5 finished sample pieces within 7 days — free, with one light round of feedback — so you can judge the quality before committing.

**Can you support multiple properties or a whole group?**
Yes. The hotel packages cover up to 3 properties; for 5+ we build a custom group package with one plan and one point of contact.

**How many rounds of revisions are included?**
One light round of feedback per cycle, so things stay efficient and on schedule.

**Who actually does the work?**
Led by Devon Archer, Archer Design operates as a lean hospitality creative studio — strategy, design, motion, and SEO support without agency bloat.

## 6. Mobile layout notes

- Single column throughout; CTAs full-width and thumb-reachable. Primary CTA repeats after hero, after packages, and in the final CTA.
- Hero: scale headline down (keep the serif), stack CTAs vertically, keep the gold glow subtle to protect text contrast.
- Proof strip: 2-column grid of metrics (or a horizontal swipe), numbers stay prominent.
- Portfolio: 1-column cards, lazy-loaded images.
- Cost comparison: stack the two cards (Archer card second so it "wins" the scroll).
- Packages: stacked cards grouped by category with a sticky category label; featured Bundle keeps its gold border.
- Sticky header collapses to logo + a single "7-Day Trial" button; nav in a menu.
- Keep tap targets ≥ 44px; maintain ivory-on-dark contrast; reduce glow/grain intensity for performance.

## 7. Existing site copy to remove or rewrite

- **"Available for Remote, Part-Time & Fractional Contract Roles"** → **"Available for monthly hospitality creative support."**
- **Personal-portfolio framing around "Devon Archer"** → lead with **"Archer Design"** as the studio; keep Devon as **founder/creative lead** (add the studio line in the final CTA / about area).
- **Any generic agency/job-seeker language** (e.g. "open to opportunities," "freelancer," "I help brands…") → studio voice: "Archer Design helps hospitality teams…"
- **Banned words to scrub anywhere on the site:** game-changer, revolutionary, unlock, elevate, synergy, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, next-level. Don't overuse "AI."
- **Avoid cheap framing** ("affordable freelancer," "budget option") → use **"lower-overhead," "efficient," "a fraction of the cost of an in-house team."**
- Replace any single-service portfolio focus with the **hospitality-specific package + trial** structure above.
- Keep the proof metrics, but present them as a **studio results strip**, not a personal résumé stat.
