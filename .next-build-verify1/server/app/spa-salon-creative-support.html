FAQ

Good questions.

Do you work with hotel spas and standalone spas?+

Both. Hotel spas, day spas, resort wellness centers, med spas, salons, and standalone beauty studios all work on the same model. The deliverables shift to match your service menu and seasonal rhythm.

What do you need from us each month?+

Whatever's coming up: a new treatment, a seasonal offer, a practitioner feature, an upcoming promotion. Plus any photos you have. We handle the planning, design, captions, motion, and Google Business posts from there.

Can you keep the aesthetic calm and premium?+

That's the goal. Wellness content tends to go wrong when it gets busy or generic. We build everything to feel considered: clean layouts, muted luxury tones, and copy that doesn't oversell.

What are the 4 motion / promo assets?+

Short animated graphics or video edits, the kind that work well as Reels, Stories, or paid social. We scope the specific formats during onboarding based on your content.

Is there a commitment?+

Most partnerships start month-to-month. Ask us about terms when you reach out, and we'll give you a direct answer based on what you need.
