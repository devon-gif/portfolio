# Archer Design — Message Templates

> Starting skeletons only. **Never paste verbatim** — the Outreach Writer varies these every time. `[brackets]` = fill from the lead brief.

## Cold LinkedIn — direct buyer
> Hey [Name] — been looking at [Property]'s page, [specific real detail]. Looks like the content's been a bit on/off lately. I do creative for a few hotels ([relevant client]) — basically turn the photos you already have into a steady stream of finished posts and Reels without you having to hire anyone. Happy to send 5 free pieces so you can see the style. No pressure either way.

## Super-short
> Hey [Name] — love [specific detail] at [Property]. I turn existing hotel photos into finished social content for a few properties. Want me to send 5 free samples for [Property]?

## Right-person ask (router / "pass to marketing")
> Appreciate that! Could you point me to who runs creative/social for [Property]? Happy to send them 5 free sample pieces so they've got something concrete instead of just a pitch.

## Email — direct buyer
> **Subject:** 5 free pieces for [Property]?
>
> Hi [Name],
> [Specific real observation about their content]. I work with a few hotels ([relevant client]) turning the photos and assets they already have into consistent social content — without them adding an in-house designer.
> Happy to make 5 finished pieces for [Property] this week, free, so you can see the fit. Portfolio's here if useful: https://devonarcher.framer.website/
> No worries if the timing's off.
> — Devon

## Partner / referral
> Hey [Name] — you work with a lot of [properties] that probably need better content but don't want to hire a full creative team. I run that as an outside service. If you intro me to one and it becomes a client, I cut you in for the life of the contract — I handle all the creative. Worth a quick chat?

## Hiring-signal / contract alternative
> Hi [Name] — saw [Company] is hiring for [role]. Happy to apply formally, but I also wanted to flag the contract/retainer option in case flexible creative support fits better than adding full-time headcount. I do this for a few hotels already — portfolio here: https://devonarcher.framer.website/

## Follow-up — no response (soft bump)
> Hey [Name] — no worries if this isn't a priority. If it helps, I can just send over 5 free pieces for [Property] so you've got something real to look at. Either way, all good.

## "What do you charge?"
> Good question — it usually works out to a lot less than a part-time hire once you factor in salary/benefits. Before numbers though, easiest is for me to send 5 free pieces so you can see the quality. Want me to?

## Banned phrases (never use)
revolutionary, game-changer, unlock, elevate, leverage, synergy, "I hope this finds you well," "I wanted to reach out," "pick your brain," "Dear [Name],"
