# Template — LinkedIn Comment

> Read `voice-profile.md` + `anti-ai-writing-style.md` first. Add a real thought first; only nod to services if natural. Never hijack the post.

## No-pitch (default)
> [Genuine, specific reaction to the actual post] + [one real insight or question that adds to it].
Example: "This is so real — the seasonal content gap hits every property in shoulder season. The teams that batch a month of assets in one sitting seem to ride it out best."

## Light-services version
> [Real thought first.] + one light phrase: "this is basically what I do for a few hotels — happy to share how I approach it."

## Shorter version
> [One sharp, specific reaction. No pitch.]

## Suggested follow-up (DM after commenting)
> Hey [Name] — your post on [topic] hit home. I help a few hotels with exactly this (turning existing photos into consistent content without adding a hire). No pitch — just thought it was relevant. Happy to share what's worked if useful.

## Notes
Never drop a link in a comment. One light services mention max. Sound like a peer.
