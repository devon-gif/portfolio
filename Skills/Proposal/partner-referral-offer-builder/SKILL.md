# Hospitality Partner / Referral Offer Builder

## Purpose
Recruit referral partners who introduce hospitality properties to Archer Design. The offer: a recurring percentage of payments for the life of any contract that results from their intro, while Devon handles all creative strategy, design, motion, delivery, and client support.

## When to use it
- A lead is classified as a partner/referral source.
- Devon wants to build a referral network of hospitality-adjacent pros.

## What it should ask me for
1. Partner name, type (consultant / recruiter / photographer / PR / advisor / agency), and how they connect to hospitality.
2. The kind of properties they touch.
3. Channel (LinkedIn / email) and any prior relationship.

## What files/context it should read first
0. **Read these three first, every time:** `ABOUT ME/about-me.md` (identity + settled decisions), `ABOUT ME/voice-profile.md` (how Devon sounds), `ABOUT ME/anti-ai-writing-style.md` (banned/avoid list).
1. `ABOUT ME/Brand Context/ARCHER-DESIGN-BRAND-CONTEXT.md` — partner structure, tone, what I handle.
2. `crm/CRM-SCHEMA.md` — partner/referral tracking fields.

## Exact instructions for the Skill
1. Read the Brand Context File and use the partner structure: recurring % of payments for the life of the contract; Devon does all the creative work and client support.
2. Frame the partner's role as effortless — just the warm intro; everything else is handled.
3. Keep commission % as a placeholder Devon confirms per partner; explain it simply and emphasize it's recurring, not one-time.
4. Write short and warmer partner messages plus an email version and a follow-up.
5. Provide vetting questions to ask before formalizing, and a simple plain-language agreement outline.

## Output format
```
PARTNER OFFER — [Partner / Type]
Short partner message: [text]
Warmer partner message: [text]
Email version: [subject + text]
Commission explanation: [plain-language, recurring %]
Follow-up: [text + timing]
CRM tracking fields: [partner name, type, intros made, properties referred, status, commission %, contracts active]
Questions to ask before formalizing: [bulleted]
Simple partner agreement outline: [intro defn, commission %, duration = life of contract, payment cadence, who-does-what, attribution rule, exit terms]
```

## Quality rules
- Make the partner's lift feel near-zero; the value is recurring and passive.
- Be transparent about commission being recurring for the contract's life.
- Vetting questions must surface whether they actually have property relationships.
- Tone: peer-to-peer, low-pressure, "worth a quick chat."

## What it must never do
- Never promise a specific commission % the system invented — keep it a placeholder Devon sets.
- Never imply the partner does creative work or client support.
- Never overcomplicate the agreement; keep it simple and readable.
- Never use banned phrases.

## Usage example
> **Devon:** Build a partner offer for a hotel sales consultant I met.
>
> **Skill:** [short + warmer messages framing "intro me to a property, and if it becomes a client you get a recurring cut for the life of the contract — I handle all the creative" → commission explainer → vetting questions about their property relationships → simple agreement outline → CRM fields.]
