# 7-Day / 5-Asset Trial Builder

## Purpose
Convert interest into a clear, no-risk free trial that's easy to say yes to and naturally leads into a paid month or 6-month retainer.

## When to use it
- A lead says "interested," "send me more," or "what would this look like."
- Whenever Devon wants to put the free trial on the table concretely.

## What it should ask me for
1. Lead name, property type, and what assets they likely have.
2. Any upcoming season, event, or special to anchor the 5 pieces around.
3. Which asset mix fits best (graphics / motion / promos / menu visuals / campaign).

## What files/context it should read first
0. **Read these three first, every time:** `ABOUT ME/about-me.md` (identity + settled decisions), `ABOUT ME/voice-profile.md` (how Devon sounds), `ABOUT ME/anti-ai-writing-style.md` (banned/avoid list).
1. `ABOUT ME/Brand Context/ARCHER-DESIGN-BRAND-CONTEXT.md` — exact trial structure, offers, tone.
2. `Examples/Portfolio Proof/PORTFOLIO-PROOF.md` — proof to include.
3. `Skills/Proposal/proposal-package-generator/SKILL.md` — for the paid transition handoff.

## Exact instructions for the Skill
1. Read the Brand Context File and use the trial structure exactly: free, 7 days, 5 polished assets from existing assets.
2. Tailor the 5-asset mix to the lead's property type and what's coming up for them.
3. Spell out inclusions, exclusions, what's needed from them, timeline, and approval clearly so there are no surprises.
4. Make the ask tiny: assets access + brand basics + a note on what's next.
5. Define the transition to paid (1-month pilot or 6-month retainer) before the trial even starts, so it's a natural next step.
6. Write the propose message, email version, and the post-delivery follow-up.

## Output format
```
TRIAL OFFER — [Lead]
Trial description: [2–3 sentences]
What's included: [the 5-asset mix, tailored]
What's NOT included: [shoots, ad mgmt, account mgmt, unlimited revisions]
What I need from the client: [assets, brand files, a note on what's coming up]
Timeline: [7 days, with a simple day-by-day]
Approval process: [one light feedback round → final files]
Transition to paid: [how the pilot/retainer follows]
Propose message (LinkedIn): [text]
Email version: [subject + text]
Post-delivery follow-up: [text + timing]
```

## Quality rules
- The ask on the client must stay tiny — never make the free trial feel like work.
- The 5-asset mix must be specific to their property and season, not generic.
- Cost-savings / bandwidth framing in the propose + transition language.
- The paid path is mentioned early and softly, never as a bait-and-switch.

## What it must never do
- Never promise original shoots, ad management, or unlimited revisions in the trial.
- Never exceed 5 assets or 7 days "to be nice" — protect the scope.
- Never quote hard prices in the trial offer (that's the Proposal Generator's job).
- Never use banned phrases or sound desperate.

## Usage example
> **Devon:** Build the trial for a spa that's interested, summer wellness season coming up.
>
> **Skill:** [5-asset mix = 2 social graphics for summer treatments, 1 short-form motion piece, 1 promo for a seasonal package, 1 campaign graphic; tiny ask; 7-day timeline; transition into the Spa/Wellness retainer; propose message + email + post-delivery follow-up.]
