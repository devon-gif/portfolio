# Proposal Templates

> Reusable proposal scaffolds. The Proposal Generator reads this + `package-pricing-notes.md`. Keep proposals simple — not agency decks.

## Standard proposal structure
```
[Property] × Archer Design — Creative Partnership

The gap: [1–2 sentences in their own language about their content situation]
What I'd do: [the package, plainly]
Monthly output: [concrete # + types]
Timeline & workflow: [cadence + one feedback round → delivery]
What I need from you: [assets, brand basics]
Investment: $[placeholder — confirm] — vs. ~$[hire cost] for an in-house hire
Terms to confirm: [usage rights, revisions, contract length]
Next step: [free trial or start date]
```

## Short proposal message (to accompany)
> Hey [Name] — put together a simple plan for [Property]. Short version: [package] gets you about [#] finished pieces a month, on-brand, without adding a hire — works out to a lot less than bringing someone on. Want me to send the one-pager?

## Pilot → retainer framing
Lead with the pilot or trial; present the 6-month retainer as the obvious continuation once quality is proven.

## Multi-property framing
For groups: emphasize consistency across properties + one point of contact instead of staffing each location.
