# Objection Responses

> Canonical responses to common objections. The Follow-Up Generator reads this. Update with lines that work.

## "I'll pass this to marketing"
Door, not a wall. Thank + right-person ask + offer free samples so the handoff has substance.
> Appreciate that! Could you point me to who runs creative/social for [Property]? I'll send them 5 free sample pieces so they've got something real to look at.

## "We already have a team"
Position as overflow/bandwidth backup, not a replacement.
> Totally — not trying to replace anyone. I work as overflow for in-house teams when they're slammed or want more output without another hire. Happy to be on file + send a few free pieces if useful.

## "No budget"
The trial is free; plant the savings seed.
> No problem — the trial doesn't cost anything. And when budget does come up, this usually runs well under what a part-time creative hire costs. Want me to send 5 free pieces in the meantime?

## "Circle back later"
Agree, pin a date, no pressure.
> Sounds good — want me to check back around [specific date]? No pressure, I'll keep it light.

## "Who have you worked with?"
Name relevant proof + portfolio.
> Sure — currently Hotel Indigo Pittsburgh, some Hampton properties, plus restaurant and spa work. Portfolio's here: https://devonarcher.framer.website/

## "What do you charge?"
Value/savings framing, then pivot to the free trial before hard numbers.
> Depends on output, but it usually comes in well under a part-time hire once you count salary/benefits. Easiest first step is 5 free pieces so you can judge the quality — want me to send those?

## "Not interested / no thanks"
Graceful exit, leave the door open.
> All good — appreciate you taking a look. If things change or content ever becomes a headache, I'm around.
