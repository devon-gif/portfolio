# Partner / Referral Offer

> Canonical partner terms. The Partner/Referral Offer Builder reads this.

## The offer
A partner who introduces a hotel/restaurant/spa/event property that becomes a **paid client** earns a **recurring percentage of payments for the life of that contract**. Devon handles all creative strategy, design, motion, delivery, and client support. The partner just makes the warm intro.

## Ideal partners
Hotel consultants, sales consultants, recruiters, task-force professionals, hospitality advisors, photographers, PR people, agencies without in-house creative.

## Key points
- Commission is **recurring**, not one-time — paid for as long as the client pays.
- Commission **%** is a placeholder Devon confirms per partner (`[confirm %]`).
- The partner does zero delivery work.
- Attribution: the intro must be a genuine warm introduction Devon wouldn't otherwise have.

## Vetting questions (before formalizing)
1. What kinds of properties do you work with / have relationships with?
2. How many could realistically use ongoing creative help?
3. How do you usually make intros (email, in-person, call)?
4. Are you open to a simple recurring-commission arrangement?
5. Any conflicts (do you offer creative yourself)?

## Simple agreement outline
- **Intro defined:** a warm introduction to a named property decision-maker.
- **Commission:** [confirm %] of net payments from that client.
- **Duration:** life of the contract (incl. renewals tied to the original intro).
- **Payment cadence:** monthly/quarterly after Devon is paid.
- **Who does what:** Devon = all creative + client support; partner = intro only.
- **Attribution rule:** first genuine intro to a property gets credit.
- **Exit:** either side can stop making/accepting new intros anytime; existing contract commissions continue.
