# Lead Research Notes

> Working log of researched leads. The Lead Researcher Skill appends briefs here; Devon adds notes. Keeps research from being redone.

## How to use
- One entry per lead. Newest on top.
- Paste the LEAD BRIEF output, then add manual notes below it.
- Update the status line when things change.

## Template
```
### [Company] — [date] — Priority [n]/10 — Status: [CRM status]
Category: [..] | Lead type: [..] | Source: [..]
Brief: [paste Lead Researcher output]
Their language: "[verbatim quote]"
Best angle: [..]
Notes: [manual observations]
Next action: [..]
```
