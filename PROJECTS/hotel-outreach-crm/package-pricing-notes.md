> Internal pricing reference. The Proposal Generator + Outreach Writer read this. These are the **current live prices** (set 2026-06). Always frame as savings vs. an in-house hire. The free 7-day/5-asset trial is a warm-lead tool — **do not auto-offer it to every cold lead.**

## The savings anchor
A full-time creative/social hire ≈ salary + benefits + payroll tax + recruiting + management + ramp time (often $70K–$110K+/yr loaded). Even a part-time hire carries overhead. Archer Design replaces most of that output at a fraction of the loaded cost, with no HR burden. Always anchor proposals to this.
