# Free Trial Offer — 7-Day / 5-Asset

> Canonical trial spec. The Trial Builder reads this. Do not redesign the structure.

## The offer
A **free** test, delivered in **7 days**: **5 polished creative assets** built from the client's *existing* property photos, F&B/event content, seasonal campaigns, or brand assets.

## Asset mix (pick 5, tailored to property + season)
Social graphics · short-form motion pieces · event promos · menu/promotional visuals · campaign graphics.

## What's included
- 5 finished, ready-to-post assets, on-brand.
- One light round of feedback.
- Final files delivered.

## What's NOT included
- Original photo/video shoots.
- Paid-ad management.
- Account management.
- Unlimited revisions.

## What I need from the client
- Access to existing photos/assets.
- Brand basics (logo, colors, fonts) if they have them.
- A quick note on what's coming up (a season, event, or special).

## Timeline (7 days)
- Day 1: receive assets + brand basics.
- Days 2–5: design/build the 5 pieces.
- Day 6: deliver drafts, one feedback round.
- Day 7: final files.

## Approval process
One light feedback round → final files. Keep it frictionless.

## Transition to paid
Mention early and softly. After delivery: move to a **1-month paid pilot** or **6-month retainer**. The trial proves quality; the retainer is the savings-vs-hire play.
